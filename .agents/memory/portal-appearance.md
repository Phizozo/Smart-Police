name: Portal appearance
description: User-selected appearance for Admin, Officer and Citizen interfaces.
---

Use ashy-grey surfaces and black text for the Admin, Officer and Citizen interfaces.
Citizen Dashboard statistics have user-requested grey, pastel pink, glass-blue, purple and light-red accents; its emergency SOS button is red.
The public Home page uses liquid glass separately from the portals.
The user also wants plain-language, first-use-friendly UX across all three portals, with student users in mind.
Admin role and Crime stats graphs use pink, yellow and purple. Cases by status uses blue and purple. Their panels and text retain the grey-and-black portal theme.

**Why:** The user replaced the earlier navy/blue portal appearance with ashy grey and black text, then requested specific Home, Admin graph and Citizen Dashboard colour exceptions, plus plain-language student-friendly use across all three portals.

**How to apply:** Apply this to role authentication, onboarding, navigation, forms, dashboards, tables and viewer controls. Keep text readable on light buttons/status surfaces; do not recolour uploaded documents or camera content.
